# Deposit into DAMMstable

DAMMstable takes **USD₮0 on Arbitrum One**. You deposit through the Lagoon app. Your deposit settles at the fund's next NAV, and then you claim DAMMstable shares.

## Deposit

1. **Email us.** Write to [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance?subject=DAMMstable%20deposit) with the wallet address you plan to use. Wait for our reply before you send funds.
2. **Fund your wallet.** Hold the USD₮0 you want to deposit on Arbitrum One, plus a little ETH for gas.
3. **Open the fund.** Go to [DAMM Capital on Lagoon](https://app.lagoon.finance/?curator=curator%3Adamm-capital) and select **DAMM Stablecoin Fund (DAMMstable)** on Arbitrum. Or open the [vault page directly](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/42161/0xe5d6eb448ac5a762c1ebe8cd1692b9cd08025176). Check that the vault address matches the one above.
4. **Connect and enter the amount.** Connect your wallet and enter how much USD₮0 to deposit.
5. **Approve, then request.** Approve USD₮0 for the vault, then confirm the deposit request. Your USD₮0 moves into the vault's pending area.
6. **Wait for settlement.** The request stays pending until DAMM's next NAV settlement. It earns no yield while pending. You can cancel it in the app before settlement.
7. **Claim your shares.** After settlement, your DAMMstable shares are ready. Claim them in the app. They earn yield from settlement onward, claimed or not.

## Redeem

1. On the fund's Lagoon page, choose to redeem and enter how many shares to redeem.
2. Confirm the redemption request. It **cannot be cancelled** once sent.
3. After the next settlement, claim your USD₮0 in the app.

DAMMstable offers T+1 liquidity when needed. See [DAMMstable terms](../funds/dammstable-arbitrum.mdx#terms-and-fees).
