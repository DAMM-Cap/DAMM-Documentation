# Deposit into DAMMeth

DAMMeth takes **ETH on Ethereum mainnet**. The vault's asset is WETH (wrapped ETH). You deposit through the Lagoon app. Your deposit settles at the fund's next NAV, and then you claim DAMMeth shares.

## Deposit

1. **Email us.** Write to [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance?subject=DAMMeth%20deposit) with the wallet address you plan to use. Wait for our reply before you send funds.
2. **Fund your wallet.** Hold the ETH or WETH you want to deposit on Ethereum, plus extra ETH for gas.
3. **Open the fund.** Go to [DAMM Capital on Lagoon](https://app.lagoon.finance/?curator=curator%3Adamm-capital) and select **DAMM Ethereum Fund (DAMMeth)** on Ethereum. Or open the [vault page directly](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x3c63f3ce75dc83735745cf4e86b63414d95ee355). Check that the vault address matches the one above.
4. **Connect and enter the amount.** Connect your wallet and enter how much to deposit.
5. **Approve, then request.** If you deposit WETH, approve it for the vault first. Then confirm the deposit request. Your deposit moves into the vault's pending area.
6. **Wait for settlement.** The request stays pending until DAMM's next NAV settlement. It earns no yield while pending. You can cancel it in the app before settlement.
7. **Claim your shares.** After settlement, your DAMMeth shares are ready. Claim them in the app. They earn yield from settlement onward, claimed or not.

## Redeem

1. On the fund's Lagoon page, choose to redeem and enter how many shares to redeem.
2. Confirm the redemption request. It **cannot be cancelled** once sent.
3. After the next settlement, claim your WETH in the app.

DAMMeth offers T+1 liquidity when needed. See [DAMMeth terms](../funds/dammeth.mdx#terms-and-fees).

:::tip Measured in ETH
DAMMeth is built to grow your ETH. Judge it by how many ETH your shares are worth, not their USD value.
:::
