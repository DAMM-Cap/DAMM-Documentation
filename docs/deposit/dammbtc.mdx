# Request access to DAMMbtc

DAMMbtc is live, and **new subscriptions are capped**. They are handled by request, so there is no self-serve deposit flow for this fund.

<DepositNotice fund="DAMMbtc" />

## How to request access

1. **Email us.** Write to [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance?subject=DAMMbtc%20access%20request) with the subject "DAMMbtc access request". Include the wallet address you would use.
2. **Wait for our reply.** Don't send WBTC to the vault until we have confirmed. Only approved addresses can deposit.
3. **If we confirm your subscription,** the deposit follows the same request, settlement and claim flow as the other DAMM funds, in WBTC on Ethereum. See [How to deposit](./index.mdx#how-a-deposit-works).

## Already invested?

Redemptions work as usual. On the fund's [Lagoon page](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x7ededf832b5c9d8afa8f7365936100581a6db756), request a redemption with your shares. After the next NAV settlement, claim your WBTC. A redemption request cannot be cancelled once sent.

Read the [DAMMbtc fund page](../funds/dammbtc.mdx) for the strategy, fees and wrapped-asset risk.
