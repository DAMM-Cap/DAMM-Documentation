# Overview

**DAMM Capital** is a **DeFi firm** and **quantitative digital asset manager** that helps fintechs and institutions integrate DeFi — with best-in-class security and mathematical rigor. We build and operate quantitative financial infrastructure on-chain: researching, building, and deploying financial vehicles that execute autonomously through smart contracts — transparent, permissionless, and fully auditable.

![DAMM Capital banner](/img/overview-banner.png)

At the core of our vision is **Decentralized Autonomous Money Management (DAMM)** — a framework for how capital should be managed in a transparent, efficient, and trust-minimized way.

This documentation provides an introduction to DAMM and an in-depth look at our verticals, along with the infrastructure that powers them under the hood.
