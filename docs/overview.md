# Overview

**DAMM Capital** is a **DeFi frontier lab** focused on creating decentralized capital management solutions. Our team of engineers, quants, and asset managers is passionate about building the future of Decentralized Finance.

![DAMM Capital banner](/img/overview-banner.png)

At the core of our vision is **Decentralized Autonomous Money Management (DAMM)** — a framework for how capital should be managed in a transparent, efficient, and trust-minimized way.

This documentation provides an introduction to DAMM and an in-depth look at our verticals, along with the infrastructure that powers them under the hood.
