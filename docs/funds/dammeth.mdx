## Objective

The fund actively allocates capital across a range of yield optimization
techniques, aiming to maximize nominal returns on Ether — preserving ETH
as a long-term store of value while compounding sustainable, on-chain-native
yield.

- **ETH-native income** — returns are generated and measured in ETH; track
  performance in the fund's native share price, not its USD value.
- **Market-neutral** — correlated-pair strategies and delta-neutral
  positioning; no directional exposure beyond ETH itself.
- **Capital preservation** — blue-chip protocols only, with on-chain
  permission policies constraining every action the fund can take.
- **Diversification** — exposure spread across protocols and position types.

## Strategy

<img src="/img/dammeth-fund.png" alt="DAMMeth fund" width="340" />

DAMMeth deploys capital through a diversified set of strategies:

- **Algorithmic liquidity provision** using LSTs and LRTs on correlated pairs
  via DEXes like Uniswap, powered by proprietary models.
- **Overcollateralized lending** on trusted on-chain credit markets.
- **Fixed income** using zero-coupon bonds and fixed-rate protocols.
- **LST/LRT arbitrage** and redemption-based opportunities.
- **Liquid staking** for additional ETH-native yield.

The result is a transparent, partially algorithmic, discretionary capital
allocation model across blue-chip DeFi ecosystems.

[Lagoon app](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x3c63f3ce75dc83735745cf4e86b63414d95ee355).

**Your position** — connect your wallet on the fund's
[Lagoon app page](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x3c63f3ce75dc83735745cf4e86b63414d95ee355)
to see your shares, their current value, and pending requests. Fund-level NAV
and performance are published on [dammcap.finance](https://dammcap.finance).
