## Objective

The fund aims to maximize nominal returns on ether. It treats ETH as a long-term store of value and compounds sustainable, onchain-native yield on top.

- **ETH-native income.** Returns are earned and measured in ETH. Track the fund by its share price in ETH, not its USD value.
- **Market-neutral.** Correlated-pair strategies and delta-neutral positions. No directional exposure beyond ETH itself.
- **Capital preservation.** Blue-chip protocols only. Onchain permission policies ([Zodiac Roles](../funds-architecture/zodiac-roles-module.md)) limit every action DAMM's trading systems can take.
- **Diversification.** Exposure is spread across protocols and position types.

## Strategy

DAMMeth spreads capital across ETH-denominated yield sources. It targets returns above the base staking rate while staying market-neutral.

- **Algorithmic liquidity provision** on correlated LST/ETH pairs (such as wstETH, rETH and cbETH against WETH) on DEXs such as Uniswap, run by proprietary models.
- **Overcollateralized lending** on trusted onchain credit markets.
- **Collateralized borrowing.** The fund may borrow stablecoins against staked-ETH collateral on blue-chip money markets such as Spark, with conservative health factors enforced by onchain permissions.
- **Fixed yield**, using zero-coupon bonds and fixed-rate protocols.
- **LST and LRT arbitrage**, including redemption-based opportunities.
- **Liquid staking** for extra ETH-native yield.

Allocation is partly algorithmic and partly discretionary. Algorithmic tooling watches yield spreads, protocol risk and onchain conditions. All yield is generated onchain and the assets stay self-custodied at all times, so anyone can audit them.

Performance, current allocation and risk metrics are on the [DAMMeth factsheet](https://dammcap.finance/funds/dammeth).

The live fee settings are always readable onchain and through the [Lagoon API](../integrations/dammeth.mdx) (`managementFee` and `performanceFee`, in basis points).

## How to deposit

1. Email [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance?subject=DAMMeth%20deposit) before you deposit.
2. Follow the [DAMMeth deposit guide](../deposit/dammeth.mdx). It covers the request, settlement and claim steps, and how to redeem.

**Your position.** Connect your wallet on the fund's [Lagoon page](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x3c63f3ce75dc83735745cf4e86b63414d95ee355) to see your shares, their value and any pending requests.

## Fund data and integrations

Everything the fund does is public and can be queried without an API key: share price, TVL, fees, APRs and the full transaction history. It is all served by Lagoon's GraphQL API, pinned to the vault address and chain above.
