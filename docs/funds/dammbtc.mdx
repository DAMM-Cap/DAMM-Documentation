## Objective

- **BTC-native income.** Returns are earned and measured in BTC. Track the fund by its share price in BTC, not its USD value.
- **Rule-based, market-neutral execution.** Concentrated-liquidity market making between WBTC and liquid BTC-pegged tokens. No leverage, and no directional exposure beyond BTC itself.
- **Mandate enforced onchain.** Onchain permission policies (Zodiac Roles) limit where the fund can allocate. Capital can only flow into the pre-approved strategy set.
- **Fees charged once.** The fund allocates into DAMM's BTC execution vehicle, which charges no fees of its own. You pay fees only at the DAMMbtc level.

:::note Wrapped-asset risk
BTC is not native to Ethereum. DAMMbtc holds BTC through wrapped tokens (such as WBTC and tBTC). That adds risk that ETH or USD funds don't carry, including custodian or bridge failure and depegs.
:::

## Strategy

DAMMbtc currently allocates to the **DAMM BTC Algo Fund**. That fund runs rule-based concentrated liquidity between WBTC and tBTC on Uniswap (Ethereum mainnet), earning trading fees on the flow between them. Each liquidity position runs in its own isolated execution account.

As the fund grows, the mandate is designed to add carry trades against highly liquid, conservative assets on blue-chip protocols.

All yield is generated onchain and the assets stay self-custodied at all times, so anyone can audit them.
