# DAMMbtc — DAMM Bitcoin Fund

DAMMbtc is a BTC-denominated fund designed to generate Bitcoin-native yield
by deploying WBTC into rule-based, market-neutral strategies on Ethereum —
capturing on-chain yield while preserving BTC as the unit of account.

## Objective

- **BTC-native income** — returns are generated and measured in BTC; track
  performance in the fund's native share price, not its USD value.
- **Rule-based, market-neutral execution** — concentrated-liquidity market
  making between WBTC and highly liquid BTC-pegged tokens; no leverage, no
  directional exposure beyond BTC itself.
- **On-chain enforced mandate** — the fund's allocation path is constrained
  by on-chain permission policies (Zodiac Roles); capital can only flow into
  the pre-approved strategy set.
- **Fees charged once** — the fund allocates into DAMM's BTC execution
  vehicle, which charges no fees of its own; investors pay fees only at the
  DAMMbtc level.

:::note
Since BTC is non-native to Ethereum, BTC-denominated DeFi strategies
inherently carry additional wrapped-asset risk compared to ETH- or
USD-denominated equivalents.
:::

## Strategy

DAMMbtc currently allocates to the **DAMM BTC Algo Fund** — a rule-based
concentrated-liquidity market-making strategy between WBTC and highly liquid
BTC tokens (such as cbBTC and tBTC) on blue-chip DEXes, run per-pair in
isolated execution accounts. The mandate is designed to scale into carry
trades against highly liquid, conservative assets on blue-chip protocols as
the fund grows.

in basis points).

[Lagoon app](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x7ededf832b5c9d8afa8f7365936100581a6db756).

**Your position** — connect your wallet on the fund's
[Lagoon app page](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/1/0x7ededf832b5c9d8afa8f7365936100581a6db756)
to see your shares, their current value, and pending requests. Fund-level NAV
and performance are published on [dammcap.finance](https://dammcap.finance).
