# Funds

DAMM runs tokenized, non-custodial funds on [Lagoon](https://docs.lagoon.finance/overview/readme) vaults and Safe smart accounts. DAMM's trading systems deploy the capital, but only within onchain permission policies ([Zodiac Roles](../funds-architecture/zodiac-roles-module.md)). Fund shares are ERC-20 tokens priced by NAV. You enter and exit through requests that settle at the fund's NAV.

## Who can deposit

Anyone can read fund data. Only approved wallet addresses can deposit or redeem: all three funds run in Lagoon's whitelist mode. To get started, email [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance) **before** you send anything. Then follow the [How to deposit](../deposit/index.mdx) guide for your fund.

:::note Execution vehicles
Two internal funds, DAMMethAlgo and DAMMbtcAlgo, run algorithmic strategies that the flagship funds allocate into. You cannot invest in them directly. Their onchain data is public like everything else; see [Integrations](../integrations/index.mdx).
:::
