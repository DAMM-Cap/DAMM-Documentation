# DAMMeth

DAMMeth is a market-neutral ETH-denominated fund designed to deliver risk-adjusted returns by combining decentralized market-making models with diversified strategies across battle-tested DeFi infrastructure on different blockchains.

<img src="/img/dammeth-fund.png" alt="DAMMeth fund" width="340" />

The strategy actively allocates capital across a range of yield optimization techniques, aiming to maximize nominal returns on Ether while maintaining zero historical drawdowns on the fund's price per share. Investors also benefit from T+1 liquidity, if needed.

DAMMeth deploys capital through a diversified set of strategies, including: Algorithmic liquidity provision using LSTs and LRTs on correlated pairs via DEXes like Uniswap (powered by proprietary models), Overcollateralized lending on trusted onchain credit markets, Fixed-income strategies using zero-coupon bonds and fixed-rate protocols, LST/LRT arbitrage and redemption-based opportunities, Liquid staking for additional ETH-native yield.

The result is a transparent, partially algorithmic, and discretionary capital allocation model—built to preserve Ether as a long-term store of value while compounding sustainable, onchain-native yield across blue-chip DeFi ecosystems.
