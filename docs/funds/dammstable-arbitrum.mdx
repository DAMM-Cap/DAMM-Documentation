## Objective

The fund aims to maximize nominal returns on stablecoins while protecting principal.

- **Income.** Diversified DeFi yield from lending, liquidity provision and fixed-yield strategies.
- **Capital preservation.** Only blue-chip protocols with enough liquidity, solvency and operational strength. Onchain permission policies ([Zodiac Roles](../funds-architecture/zodiac-roles-module.md)) limit every action DAMM's trading systems can take.
- **Diversification.** Exposure is spread across protocols, position types and chains, so no single source of return or risk dominates.
- **Liquidity.** Request-based deposits and redemptions, with T+1 processing when needed.

## Strategy

DAMMstable spreads capital across onchain yield sources. It targets stable USD returns with low correlation to the wider crypto market.

- **Overcollateralized lending** on trusted onchain credit markets.
- **Algorithmic liquidity provision** in concentrated-liquidity pools on DEXs such as Uniswap, run by in-house models.
- **Fixed yield and yield trading**, using zero-coupon bonds and fixed-rate protocols.

An internal risk-adjusted framework drives allocation. Algorithmic tooling supports it by watching yield spreads, protocol liquidity and onchain conditions in real time. All yield is generated onchain, and the assets stay onchain at all times, so anyone can audit them.

The fund started as a fully algorithmic mandate, which is now one strategy inside it. That mandate was the subject of an ecosystem-impact report for the Uniswap Foundation: [DAMMstable Ecosystem Impact](https://bubble-scilla-7a7.notion.site/DAMMstable-Ecosystem-Impact-264fb660f37c8019a8abf5eec16e8b26).

Performance, current allocation and risk metrics are on the [DAMMstable factsheet](https://dammcap.finance/funds/dammstable).

## How to deposit

1. Email [team@dammcap.finance](mailto:team@dammcap.finance?subject=DAMMstable%20deposit) before you deposit.
2. Follow the [DAMMstable deposit guide](../deposit/dammstable.mdx). It covers the request, settlement and claim steps, and how to redeem.

**Your position.** Connect your wallet on the fund's [Lagoon page](https://app.lagoon.finance/vault/42161/0xe5d6eb448ac5a762c1ebe8cd1692b9cd08025176) to see your shares, their value and any pending requests.

Everything the fund does is public and can be queried without an API key: share price, TVL, fees, APRs and the full transaction history. It is all served by Lagoon's GraphQL API, pinned to the vault address and chain above.
