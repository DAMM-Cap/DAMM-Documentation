## Objective

The fund actively allocates capital across a range of yield optimization
techniques, aiming to maximize nominal returns on stablecoins.

- **Income generation** — diversified DeFi yield: algorithmic liquidity
  provision, overcollateralized lending, and fixed-income strategies.
- **Capital preservation** — allocations restricted to blue-chip protocols
  with adequate liquidity, solvency, and operational robustness; on-chain
  permission policies constrain every action the fund can take.
- **Diversification** — exposure spread across protocols, position types, and
  chains to reduce reliance on any single source of return or risk.
- **Liquidity** — request-based subscriptions and redemptions with T+1
  processing when needed.

## Strategy

DAMMstable deploys capital through a diversified set of strategies:

- **Algorithmic liquidity provision** on DEXes like Uniswap, powered by
  in-house models.
- **Overcollateralized lending** via trusted on-chain credit protocols.
- **Fixed income** using zero-coupon bonds and fixed-rate lending protocols.

The result is a transparent, partially algorithmic, discretionary capital
allocation model — built to preserve principal while compounding stable,
on-chain-native yield across blue-chip DeFi ecosystems.

The fund's original fully algorithmic mandate (now one strategy within the
fund) was the subject of an ecosystem-impact report prepared for the Uniswap
Foundation: [DAMMstable Ecosystem Impact](https://bubble-scilla-7a7.notion.site/DAMMstable-Ecosystem-Impact-264fb660f37c8019a8abf5eec16e8b26).
